## 2. Suggested weekend schedule

Saturday is the busiest day, and Friday is usually the calmest. Choose based on how many days you can take off.

**Option A: Friday venue day (fewer crowds, needs Friday off)**
| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| Thu Feb 18 | Drive in during the evening and check in near Pasadena |
| **Fri Feb 19** | **Venue day** at the Rose Bowl, 9–5. Mega Night 5–7. Dinner in Old Pasadena |
| Sat Feb 20 | Citywide play (with Extra Day): Santa Monica → Venice, or DTLA → Little Tokyo |

## 3. HOT locations in LA for playing

The citywide part last year covered the greater LA area. These spots have the most PokéStops, Gyms and Routes and are the best places to play outside the venue. **Alola tip:** expect a lot of Water and beach-themed Pokémon, so the beach and pier spots should be extra good. Spawn details won't be confirmed until Niantic announces them.

### Pasadena (closest to the venue)
| Spot | Why it's good |
|---|---|
| **Old Pasadena / Colorado Blvd** | Very dense with stops along the Route 66 trail. Restaurants and boba nearby. The best place to go after the event |
| **Pasadena City Hall** (100 N Garfield Ave) | Landmark gym. The Paseo-to-City-Hall route is popular |
| **Central Park** (275 S Raymond Ave) | Stops and gyms in a cluster, a good place to rest |
| **Memorial Park** (85 E Holly St) | Right at the Memorial Park Metro stop |
| **Rose Bowl loop / Arroyo Seco** | 3-mile loop around the stadium that's good for hatching eggs |
| **Caltech campus** | Quiet campus packed with stops |

### Los Angeles core
| Spot | Why it's good |
|---|---|
| **Santa Monica Pier + Third Street Promenade** | Probably the best hotspot in SoCal: beach spawns and stops on every block |
| **Venice Beach Boardwalk** | Beach spawns with lots of stops. Walk to it from Santa Monica |
| **Little Tokyo / Japanese Village Plaza** | Dense cluster on the Metro A Line, with great food |
| **Grand Park → Civic Center (DTLA)** | Long park with many stops and gyms, also on the A Line |
| **Griffith Observatory / Griffith Park** | Landmark spot with good walking for egg hatching. Parking fills up early |
| **Echo Park Lake** | Lake loop that's good for Water-type spawns |
| **Hollywood Walk of Fame / Hollywood & Highland** | Lots of stops and tourists |
| **Universal CityWalk** | Free to walk, many stops, garage parking |
| **The Grove / Farmers Market** | Easy parking with a dense cluster of stops |
| **UCLA / USC campuses** | Big campuses packed with stops. Quieter on weekends |

### Worth the drive
| Spot | Why it's good |
|---|---|
| **Long Beach: Shoreline Village / Rainbow Harbor / Aquarium** | Waterfront spawns and a walkable harbor loop |
| **Huntington Beach Pier + Main St** | Pier and beach spawns |
| **Downtown Disney (Anaheim)** | Free to enter with lots of stops. Parking costs money |
| **Irvine Spectrum** | Outdoor mall packed with stops |

## 4. Where to stay

Book a **refundable** room **now** and rebook if you find a lower price later. Hotels close to the Rose Bowl sell out and get more expensive for Tour weekend. Pasadena usually averages about $160/night, but hotels near the Rose Bowl average about $260 and will cost more on event weekend.

| Area | Price range* | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Old Pasadena / Downtown Pasadena** (Westin, Hilton, Sheraton, Hotel Constance, Courtyard) | $$$ ($250–400+) | You can walk to restaurants and spots and drive or rideshare 5–10 min to the Rose Bowl. Easiest choice |
| **East Colorado Blvd, Pasadena** (Best Western Royale, Saga Motor Hotel, Howard Johnson, Ramada, Holiday Inn Express) | $$ ($150–250) | The best value, about 10 min to the Rose Bowl with free parking at most |
| **Arcadia / Monrovia / Duarte** (cluster of chain hotels on the Metro A Line) | $–$$ ($120–200) | Cheaper, and you can take the Metro straight to Pasadena |
| **Glendale / Burbank** | $$ ($150–250) | 10–15 min drive with many chain hotels. Good base for Griffith and Universal |
| **DTLA / Little Tokyo** | $$–$$$ | Puts you in a hotspot with the A Line to Pasadena. Parking costs $40+/night |
| **The Langham Huntington** | $$$$ ($450+) | Luxury option |

*These are estimated event-weekend prices, so check live rates. Splitting a room with 2–4 friends cuts this a lot.

## 5. Getting there and around

### Driving and parking *(2026 pricing)*
- **Rose Bowl parking: $34 bought ahead / $40 on the day.** Spots are limited, so buy parking when you buy your ticket
- Accessible (ADA) parking costs the same and is first-come.
- No overnight RVs.

### Metro (cheapest way, no parking stress)
- **Metro A Line** to **Memorial Park** station in Pasadena, about $1.75 a ride. From there the Rose Bowl is about 2 miles. Walk, bike or take a rideshare.
- Last year Niantic listed A Line stations from Pico, 7th St/Metro Center, Grand Ave Arts, Historic Broadway and Little Tokyo.

### Rideshare
- Drop-off is at the **southeast corner of Lot H** *(2026)*. Prices will surge at 5 PM and after Mega Night, so walk toward Old Pasadena before requesting a ride.

### Venue walking
- The venue footprint is **about 1.3 miles on grass and natural ground**. No golf carts or wheelchairs are provided.

## 6. Gas cost

**Current prices (week of Sep 28, 2026):** LA regular **$6.17/gal**, California average **$6.19**, US average **$4.47**.
California prices usually fall a bit by February when winter-blend gas returns, but **plan for $6.00/gal to be safe**.

**Formula:** `(round-trip miles + ~80 mi of local LA driving) ÷ your car's MPG × $/gal`

Estimates at **25 MPG and $6.00/gal**. Distances to Pasadena are approximate:

| Driving from | One way | Total miles (RT + 80 local) | Gallons | **Gas cost** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inland Empire (Riverside) | ~50 mi | ~180 | 7.2 | **~$43** |
| Bakersfield | ~110 mi | ~300 | 12 | **~$72** |
| San Diego | ~135 mi | ~350 | 14 | **~$84** |
| Fresno | ~210 mi | ~500 | 20 | **~$120** |
| Las Vegas | ~255 mi | ~590 | 23.6 | **~$142** (less if you fill up in NV) |
| Phoenix | ~365 mi | ~810 | 32.4 | **~$194** (less if you fill up in AZ) |
| San Francisco / Bay Area | ~380 mi | ~840 | 33.6 | **~$202** |

**Ways to save:**
- If you're coming from out of state, fill up before entering California. Gas in Nevada and Arizona is usually $1.50–2.00/gal cheaper.
- Costco and Arco are usually the cheapest in LA. Check GasBuddy.
- Use Metro on hotspot days so you don't pay $20–40 for parking at each stop.

## 8. What to bring

**Bag rules at the venue *(2026)*:** only **clear bags** up to 12"×6"×12", a 1-gallon freezer bag, or a small clutch (4.5"×6.5"). **No backpacks, fanny packs, mesh or tinted bags.**

- [ ] Phone with the game updated, plus a fully charged phone-size **power bank** (one is allowed inside) and cable
- [ ] Spare battery or car charger for citywide days
- [ ] Auto-catcher (Go Plus+ / Gotcha), if you use one
- [ ] Refillable water bottle (allowed)
- [ ] Sunscreen, hat and sunglasses. There's very little shade
- [ ] Layers: February in Pasadena is about 68°F in the day and 45°F at night, with a chance of rain. Mega Night gets cold
- [ ] Comfortable shoes, since you'll walk 10+ miles a day
- [ ] Rain poncho
- [ ] Parking pass and ticket confirmation (screenshot them)
- [ ] Downloaded offline Google Map of LA
- [ ] Clear out Pokémon and item storage before you go
